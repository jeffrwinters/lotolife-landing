# LOTO Life influencer research

Updated November 25, 2026. Preliminary public-source research; no outreach, quotes, bookings, or verified sales results.

## Current brief

Adult swimwear, boating, attractive lake lifestyle and lake-party content. Reach potential customers in Springfield (Missouri), Jefferson City, Kansas City, St. Louis, and Omaha. Audience geography matters more than the creator's residence. Family-travel creators are outside the current brief. Jeff Pfitzer was considered earlier and excluded at the owner's direction.

## Campaign economics and next steps

An initial discussion used a $1,000-$2,500 total test budget across two or three creators, with $250-$1,000 per creator plus product/shipping as planning allowances only. No candidate quoted those amounts; talent, production, posting, advertising permissions and exclusivity can change pricing. [General pricing context](https://www.shopify.com/blog/influencer-pricing).

Request recent audience-city screenshots, last 10 comparable posts' median views, sponsored story views and link clicks, past campaign sales if available, rate card, deliverables, usage rights, and competitor exclusivity. Prefer a small paid test with a unique tracked link/code and commission that fits actual margins. No sales guarantees.

Measure incremental contribution after supplier cost, shipping subsidy, processing fees, discounts, returns allowance, creator commission and campaign cost. At $10 contribution per order before fixed campaign cost, $1,000 requires 100 incremental orders to break even. Validate products, delivered margins, and checkout (including physical iPhone/Safari) before a sales campaign.
